Print it (A1 or P2S)

PETG · 0.20 mm layers · 4 walls · 15–25% gyroid
Both parts print standing · no supports
About 140 g · roughly 3–4 h for both

stl/handle.stl · stl/yoke.stl · stl/fit_test_rings.stl

You also need

  • M8 threaded rod, cut to 360 mm, + 2 nuts + 2 washers
  • 2 × hook-and-loop straps, 20 mm wide, ~20 cm
  • 1.5 mm EVA foam tape for the cups and saddles
  • Optional: a cricket bat rubber grip

Buttons while batting

A / X Run   B / Y Go back
Thumbstick click or flick: Dive (while running)
Your bottom thumb reaches down onto either faceplate. Same bat for left- and right-handers.

Fit first (15 min)

Print the two fit rings. Each should slide over the grip with a hair of play. Adjust the 4 grip numbers in the .scad file if not. Controllers shown here are simplified stand-ins.
